# Mic-first layout preview (Nov 9 2026) — a finding, not the approved layout

Eddie wants the mic / quick thoughts to be the focal point, with Reflect as a plain list of notes. Rendered from the real
app in sample-data mode with `-MockLayout 1`; without that argument, and always on a real account, the app is unchanged.

- Tab bar: This Week, Journal, mic (raised, starts recording from any screen), Prayer, Progress.
- This Week: a "latest thought" strip between the habits and the streak card; tap opens the week's journal.
- Journal: this week's entries newest first, then earlier weeks; Write at the top. Prayer becomes its own tab.

Known flaws in these shots: the tab bar's top rule runs across the mic; the mic covers a little of the list behind it.
