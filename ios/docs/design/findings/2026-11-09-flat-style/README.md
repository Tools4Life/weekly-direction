# Flat style preview (Nov 9 2026) — a finding, not an approved look

Eddie's inspiration: flat, square, outlined panels with heavy condensed caps. Rendered from the real app in sample-data
mode with `-MockStyle 1` (A) or `-MockStyle 2` (B); without that argument, and always on a real account, the app is unchanged.

- **A**: square, outlined shapes; fonts as they are (Fraunces titles, Barlow habit names).
- **B**: A plus Barlow Condensed SemiBold caps for screen titles and habit names. The journal keeps its serif in both.

Known misses in these shots: the day squares are still rounded; toggles are the system ones; no accent top bar.
